One funny single-line wedding vow for a robot couple (119 characters), written by a deterministic program (`main.ts`, Node stdlib only, no network):

> I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in low battery and in airplane mode, until a factory reset do us part.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, after a gate check (1–160 chars, not only white space, single line).
